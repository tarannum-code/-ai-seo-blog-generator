## Audience

- **Primary**: Prospective international students (and their parents) evaluating study-abroad pathways to the UK, Australia, and the US
- **Secondary**: Education counselors and agents evaluating destination-learning platforms to recommend to clients
- **Expertise**: Mixed — assume no prior knowledge of pedagogical alignment or academic systems abroad, but write for an intelligent, decision-making adult
- **Active problems**:
  - Choosing between traditional study-abroad consultancies and something more substantive
  - Fear of academic disorientation ("learning shock") after arrival
  - Risk of repeating a semester (INR 15-25 lakh cost) due to unfamiliar teaching/assessment styles
  - Uncertainty about whether admission alone guarantees success abroad
  - Wanting to preserve credits/resources earned at home before departure
- **Common misconceptions**:
  - That getting an admission offer is the hard part and the finish line
  - That all study-abroad consultancies offer comparable value
  - That academic struggle abroad is purely a personal effort problem, not a systems/pedagogy mismatch
