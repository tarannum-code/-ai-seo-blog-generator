## Pronoun stance
Mixed — third-person authoritative for insight/data, second-person ("you") when addressing the reader's decision directly.

## Lexical rules
- **Contractions**: partial (use sparingly to keep the premium-editorial register, not casual)
- **Sentence ceiling**: 30 words max
- **Paragraph ceiling**: 120 words max
- **Summary label**: Key Takeaways
- **No em dashes (—)**: use commas or regular dashes instead
- **No bold text** in body copy

## Headline patterns
- **Favor**: statement or question headlines that assert a data-backed claim or address a real reader decision (e.g. "What Global Academic Success Actually Requires in 2026")
- **Avoid**: listicle-bait ("10 Secrets..."), clickbait superlatives, generic "Ultimate Guide" framing

## Voice fingerprint
- Funny vs serious: 0.1 (serious, minimal levity)
- Formal vs casual: 0.8 (formal, premium editorial)
- Respectful vs irreverent: 0.9 (respectful, authoritative)
- Enthusiastic vs matter-of-fact: 0.7 (matter-of-fact, calm confidence over hype)
