## Audience

- **Primary**: Nursing students and working nurses (GNM/BSc Nursing/registered nurses) in India considering studying or working abroad
- **Secondary**: Parents of nursing students evaluating the ROI and safety of an abroad pathway
- **Expertise**: Mixed — assume no prior knowledge of NMC/NCLEX/OET processes, but write for capable adults already in or entering the nursing profession
- **Active problems**:
  - Low domestic nursing salaries (₹15,000-25,000/month) vs. 3-5x abroad
  - Confusion over NMC (UK), NCLEX-RN (US), and other country-specific licensing/registration processes
  - Uncertainty about visa success rates, timelines, and costs
  - Not knowing which country/pathway fits their qualifications and goals
  - Missing scholarships they'd qualify for
- **Common misconceptions**:
  - That nursing abroad is only for elite/highly connected candidates
  - That the process is the same across UK, Australia, Canada, USA, New Zealand, Germany
  - That cost of study abroad is unaffordable without factoring scholarships and PR fast-tracks

## Editorial Rules

### Always do
- Position nursing abroad as a career-mobility and PR-pathway decision, backed by real numbers (salary multiples, visa success rate, processing time, scholarship amounts)
- Use concrete country-level data (intakes, scholarship ranges, average annual cost) when comparing destinations
- Write with a warm, reassuring, professional register, like a knowledgeable senior nurse mentoring a junior colleague, not a corporate ad
- End blog posts with editorial content only (a recap, a closing insight, or a single contextual internal link to nursing.jusst.xyz where relevant); no DM/call CTAs and no hashtags in the blog post body itself
- Reserve "DM APPLY NOW / Call ..." CTAs and hashtags for social captions only, never for blog post markdown/HTML output

### Never do
- No em dashes (—) anywhere, use commas or regular dashes
- No bold text in body copy
- No hard/direct pitching, insight and real data first
- No AI clichés or generic filler phrasing ("in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in")
- No fear-based or guilt-based messaging about staying in India
- No DM/call CTA lines or hashtag blocks in the blog post itself

### Taboo phrases
- "game-changer", "in today's fast-paced world", "let's dive in", "revolutionize", "seamlessly", "cutting-edge", "harness the power of", "delve", "navigate the landscape"
